### Question 1

A hedge fund charges a 2% management fee on beginning assets and a 20% incentive fee on returns net of the management fee. Beginning assets are $100m and the gross return is 15%. The total fee is closest to:

> AUM $100m · gross return 15% · 2% mgmt · 20% incentive, net of mgmt

A. $4.60m  
B. $4.80m  
C. $5.00m  
D. $5.60m  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** Management fee = $2m. Gross gain = $15m; net of management fee = $13m. Incentive = 20% × 13 = $2.6m. Total = $4.6m.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Performance and Returns, LOS 2.c*

</details>

### Question 2

A property generates net operating income of $450,000 and comparable properties transact at a capitalisation rate of 6%. The estimated value is:

> NOI $450,000 · cap rate 6%

A. $2,700,000  
B. $7,500,000  
C. $4,500,000  
D. $27,000  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Value = NOI / cap rate = 450,000 / 0.06 = $7,500,000. Direct capitalisation assumes NOI is constant in perpetuity.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Real Estate and Infrastructure, LOS 4.c*

</details>

### Question 3

The J-curve effect in private equity refers to:

A. Rising fees over a fund's life  
B. Early negative returns followed by later positive returns  
C. The shape of the capital call schedule  
D. Persistent underperformance versus public markets  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Fees and early write-downs are recognised before exits generate gains, so reported returns are negative early and recover later.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Investments in Private Capital, LOS 3.b*

</details>

### Question 4

An illiquid strategy reports a Sharpe ratio far above comparable liquid strategies. The most likely explanation is:

A. Genuinely superior risk-adjusted skill  
B. Return smoothing from appraisal-based valuation  
C. A higher risk-free rate assumption  
D. Survivorship bias in the risk-free rate  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Stale and appraisal-based marks smooth reported returns, understating volatility and inflating the Sharpe ratio. It is a measurement artefact.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Performance and Returns, LOS 2.d*

</details>

### Question 5

Compared with a direct investment in a hedge fund, a fund-of-funds most likely offers:

A. Lower total fees and higher manager concentration  
B. Higher total fees and greater diversification  
C. Identical fees and identical diversification  
D. Lower fees and greater diversification  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** A fund-of-funds adds a second layer of fees but provides diversification across managers and access to closed funds.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Hedge Funds, LOS 6.b*

</details>

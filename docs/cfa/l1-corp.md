### Question 1

A project requires an initial outlay of $1,000,000 and generates $300,000 per year for five years. At a cost of capital of 10%, the NPV is closest to:

> Outlay 1,000,000 · CF 300,000 × 5 yrs · r = 10%

A. −$62,000  
B. +$137,000  
C. +$500,000  
D. +$1,137,000  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** The five-year annuity factor at 10% is 3.7908, so PV = 300,000 × 3.7908 = $1,137,236. NPV = 1,137,236 − 1,000,000 ≈ +$137,000.

*Reading: Corporate Issuers — Capital Investments and Capital Allocation, LOS 5.c*

</details>

### Question 2

When NPV and IRR give conflicting rankings for two mutually exclusive projects, an analyst should most appropriately:

A. Choose the project with the higher IRR  
B. Choose the project with the higher NPV  
C. Choose the project with the shorter payback  
D. Reject both projects  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** NPV measures the value added in currency terms and assumes reinvestment at the cost of capital, which is the economically defensible assumption.

*Reading: Corporate Issuers — Capital Investments and Capital Allocation, LOS 5.d*

</details>

### Question 3

A firm has a target capital structure of 40% debt and 60% equity, a pre-tax cost of debt of 6%, a cost of equity of 11% and a tax rate of 25%. Its WACC is closest to:

> w_d 40% · r_d 6% · w_e 60% · r_e 11% · t 25%

A. 8.40%  
B. 8.40% before tax only  
C. 7.80%  
D. 9.20%  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** WACC = 0.40 × 6% × (1 − 0.25) + 0.60 × 11% = 1.80% + 6.60% = 8.40%. Only the debt component is tax-affected.

*Reading: Corporate Issuers — Capital Structure, LOS 6.b*

</details>

### Question 4

A firm reports days of inventory on hand of 60, days sales outstanding of 45 and days of payables of 30. Its cash conversion cycle is:

> DOH 60 · DSO 45 · DPO 30

A. 45 days  
B. 75 days  
C. 105 days  
D. 135 days  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** CCC = DOH + DSO − DPO = 60 + 45 − 30 = 75 days. This is the length of time cash is tied up in the operating cycle.

*Reading: Corporate Issuers — Working Capital and Liquidity, LOS 4.c*

</details>

### Question 5

The conflict of interest between controlling and minority shareholders is best described as:

A. A principal–agent problem  
B. A principal–principal problem  
C. A stakeholder externality  
D. A regulatory arbitrage  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Both parties are owners, so it is a principal–principal conflict. The principal–agent problem sits between shareholders and managers.

*Reading: Corporate Issuers — Corporate Governance, LOS 3.a*

</details>

## Sample questions

These 5 questions are free in the app too. Try each one before opening the answer.

### Question 1

A member works in a jurisdiction whose regulation permits front-running client orders. The Code and Standards prohibit it. The member must:

A. Follow local law  
B. Follow the stricter Code and Standards  
C. Disclose the practice and proceed  
D. Seek written employer approval  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Members follow the most strict of applicable law, the Code and the Standards. Less strict local law never lowers the bar.

*Reading: Standard I(A) — Knowledge of the Law*

</details>

### Question 2

An analyst accepts a paid consulting role outside her firm without informing her employer. She has most likely violated:

A. Standard III(A) Loyalty, Prudence and Care  
B. Standard IV(B) Additional Compensation Arrangements  
C. Standard V(A) Diligence and Reasonable Basis  
D. No standard, because the work is outside the firm  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Additional compensation arrangements that could conflict with the employer's interest require written consent from all parties involved.

*Reading: Standard IV(B) — Additional Compensation Arrangements*

</details>

### Question 3

A member publishes a research summary that omits the fact that his firm holds an 8% beneficial ownership stake in the issuer. This most likely violates:

A. Standard VI(A) Disclosure of Conflicts  
B. Standard II(B) Market Manipulation  
C. Standard III(D) Performance Presentation  
D. Standard VII(A) Conduct as Participants  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** Beneficial ownership that could reasonably impair independence must be disclosed prominently. Omitting it breaches Standard VI(A).

*Reading: Standard VI(A) — Disclosure of Conflicts*

</details>

### Question 4

Which action best satisfies Standard V(B), Communication with Clients and Prospective Clients?

A. Presenting projections as assured outcomes  
B. Distinguishing between fact and opinion  
C. Omitting the process used to reach the conclusion  
D. Reporting only the recommendations that performed well  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Members must distinguish fact from opinion and describe the basic process by which conclusions were reached, including its limitations.

*Reading: Standard V(B) — Communication with Clients*

</details>

### Question 5

A portfolio manager recommends a security after reading a single sell-side note and performing no further work. The security subsequently performs well. The manager has:

A. Not violated the Standards, because the result was favourable  
B. Violated Standard V(A) regardless of the outcome  
C. Violated Standard III(A) only if a client lost money  
D. Violated Standard I(C) Misrepresentation  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Standard V(A) requires a reasonable and adequate basis supported by appropriate research. The outcome is irrelevant — the process is what is judged.

*Reading: Standard V(A) — Diligence and Reasonable Basis*

</details>

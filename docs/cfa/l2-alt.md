## Sample questions

These 5 questions are free in the app too. Try each one before opening the answer.

### Question 1

A commodity futures curve is in contango. An investor holding a long-only rolling futures position will most likely earn:

A. A positive roll return  
B. A negative roll return  
C. A roll return of exactly zero  
D. A roll return independent of the curve shape  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** In contango the investor sells a cheaper expiring contract and buys a more expensive deferred one, producing a negative roll return.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Introduction to Commodities, LOS 1.d*

</details>

### Question 2

A REIT reports net income of $40m, depreciation of $22m and gains on property sales of $6m. Funds from operations is:

> NI $40m · Dep $22m · Gains $6m

A. $50m  
B. $56m  
C. $62m  
D. $68m  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** FFO = NI + depreciation − gains on sales = 40 + 22 − 6 = $56m. Gains are excluded because they are not recurring operating income.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Investments in Real Estate through Publicly Traded Securities, LOS 3.c*

</details>

### Question 3

A merger arbitrage strategy has a return profile most similar to:

A. A long call on the target  
B. A short put on deal completion  
C. A long straddle  
D. A long position in the acquirer  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** The strategy earns a small steady spread when deals close and suffers large losses when they break — the payoff shape of a short put.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Hedge Fund Strategies, LOS 4.c*

</details>

### Question 4

Compared with direct real estate ownership, publicly traded REITs most likely exhibit:

A. Lower liquidity and lower short-run equity correlation  
B. Higher liquidity and higher short-run equity correlation  
C. Higher liquidity and lower short-run equity correlation  
D. Identical liquidity and correlation  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Listed vehicles trade continuously, which gives liquidity but also imports short-run equity market beta that direct property does not display.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Investments in Real Estate through Publicly Traded Securities, LOS 3.a*

</details>

### Question 5

Under the theory of storage, a market in contango is best explained by:

A. A high convenience yield  
B. High storage costs relative to the convenience yield  
C. Producer hedging pressure  
D. Backwardated inventory levels  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Contango arises when the cost of carrying inventory exceeds the convenience yield of holding the physical commodity.

*Reading: Alternative Investments — Introduction to Commodities, LOS 1.e*

</details>

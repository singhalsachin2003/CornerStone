## Sample questions

These 5 questions are free in the app too. Try each one before opening the answer.

### Question 1

An analyst assembles several individually immaterial non-public details from suppliers and customers and, combined with public filings, concludes the issuer will miss guidance. Acting on that conclusion is:

A. A violation of Standard II(A)  
B. Permitted under the mosaic theory  
C. Permitted only after disclosure to the issuer  
D. A violation of Standard V(A)  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** The mosaic theory expressly permits combining non-material non-public information with public information. No single piece was material on its own.

*Reading: Standard II(A) — Material Nonpublic Information*

</details>

### Question 2

A firm offers premium clients earlier access to research than standard clients, and discloses the tiering to all clients. Under Standard III(B) this is:

A. A violation, because all clients must receive research simultaneously  
B. Permitted, because the differential service is disclosed  
C. A violation, unless the premium fee is waived  
D. Permitted only for institutional clients  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Fair dealing does not require identical treatment. Different service levels are permissible provided they are disclosed and do not disadvantage other clients.

*Reading: Standard III(B) — Fair Dealing*

</details>

### Question 3

A manager presents a composite that excludes two terminated accounts that underperformed. This most likely violates:

A. Standard III(D) Performance Presentation  
B. Standard VI(B) Priority of Transactions  
C. Standard IV(A) Loyalty to Employer  
D. Standard I(B) Independence and Objectivity  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** Excluding terminated accounts creates survivorship bias and makes the presentation neither fair nor complete, breaching Standard III(D).

*Reading: Standard III(D) — Performance Presentation*

</details>

### Question 4

A member takes a research report prepared at her previous employer and reuses its analysis, with attribution removed, at her new firm. She has violated:

A. Standard IV(A) only  
B. Standard I(C) Misrepresentation and Standard IV(A) Loyalty  
C. Standard V(B) only  
D. No standard, because she wrote the original analysis  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** The work product belongs to the former employer, and presenting it as new work without attribution is plagiarism under Standard I(C).

*Reading: Standard I(C) — Misrepresentation; Standard IV(A) — Loyalty*

</details>

### Question 5

An investment firm allocates a hot IPO pro rata across all suitable client accounts, including accounts in which employees are beneficial owners. Under Standard VI(B), this is:

A. Acceptable if client accounts are filled before employee-owned accounts  
B. Acceptable in all circumstances  
C. A violation regardless of the allocation order  
D. Acceptable only if the employees are not portfolio managers  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** Priority of transactions requires client interests to come before those of employees. Employee-beneficial accounts may participate only after clients are satisfied.

*Reading: Standard VI(B) — Priority of Transactions*

</details>

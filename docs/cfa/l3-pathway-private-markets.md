### Question 1

A fund has paid-in capital of $100m, distributions of $40m and a remaining net asset value of $90m. TVPI is:

> PIC $100m · Distributions $40m · NAV $90m

A. 0.40×  
B. 0.90×  
C. 1.30×  
D. 2.25×  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**C.** DPI = 40/100 = 0.40×, RVPI = 90/100 = 0.90×, so TVPI = 0.40 + 0.90 = 1.30×.

*Reading: Private Markets Pathway — General Partner and Investor Perspectives*

</details>

### Question 2

Under a European (whole-fund) waterfall compared with an American (deal-by-deal) waterfall, the general partner receives carried interest:

A. Earlier, with a higher clawback risk  
B. Later, with a lower clawback risk  
C. Earlier, with a lower clawback risk  
D. At the same time under both structures  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** A whole-fund waterfall returns all contributed capital and the preferred return before any carry is paid, which delays carry and reduces clawback risk.

*Reading: Private Markets Pathway — General Partner and Investor Perspectives*

</details>

### Question 3

A general partner uses a subscription-line credit facility to delay capital calls. The most direct effect on reported performance is:

A. TVPI rises and IRR is unchanged  
B. IRR rises and TVPI is broadly unchanged or slightly lower  
C. Both IRR and TVPI rise  
D. Both measures fall  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Delaying the capital call shortens the period capital is outstanding, flattering the money-weighted IRR. Interest cost slightly reduces the multiple.

*Reading: Private Markets Pathway — General Partner and Investor Perspectives*

</details>

### Question 4

A covenant-lite loan compared with a traditional maintenance-covenant loan most likely implies:

A. Higher expected recovery for the lender  
B. Lower expected recovery, because intervention is delayed  
C. No difference in expected recovery  
D. Higher recovery but greater default probability  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Without maintenance covenants the lender cannot force early remediation, so deterioration runs further before workout, reducing recovery.

*Reading: Private Markets Pathway — Private Debt*

</details>

### Question 5

A brownfield infrastructure asset with a regulated revenue framework will most likely display:

A. Equity-like returns and high cyclical sensitivity  
B. Bond-like returns and low cyclical sensitivity  
C. Equity-like returns and low cyclical sensitivity  
D. High construction risk  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Operational assets with regulated or contracted revenue produce stable, long-duration cash flows that behave more like bonds than equities.

*Reading: Private Markets Pathway — Infrastructure*

</details>

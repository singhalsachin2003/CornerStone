### Question 1

A client has a high-priority goal three years away and a lower-priority aspirational goal thirty years away. Goals-based allocation would fund the near-term goal with:

A. A high-equity sub-portfolio to maximise expected value  
B. A low-risk sub-portfolio regardless of overall risk tolerance  
C. The same allocation as the aspirational goal  
D. Illiquid private assets for the return premium  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Each goal is funded according to its own horizon and required probability of success. A high-priority near-term goal requires low-risk assets.

*Reading: Private Wealth Pathway — Investment Planning*

</details>

### Question 2

A senior executive holds substantial employer stock and derives employment income from the same firm. The most appropriate recommendation is:

A. Increase the employer stock position to align interests  
B. Reduce the concentration because human and financial capital are correlated  
C. Hold the position because it is tax-efficient  
D. Hedge only the employment income  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Human capital and financial capital are highly correlated here, so a shock to the employer hits both simultaneously. Diversification is the remedy.

*Reading: Private Wealth Pathway — Wealth Planning*

</details>

### Question 3

An asset returns 6% annually and is taxed at 30% on an accrual basis. Over ten years the after-tax accumulation factor is closest to:

> r = 6% · t = 30% · n = 10

A. 1.34×  
B. 1.51×  
C. 1.79×  
D. 1.26×  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** After-tax return = 6% × (1 − 0.30) = 4.2%. (1.042)¹⁰ = 1.508×. Taxing only at the end would give a higher figure.

*Reading: Private Wealth Pathway — Preserving the Wealth*

</details>

### Question 4

Asset location refers to the decision about:

A. Which countries to invest in  
B. Which account type holds each asset class  
C. The proportion held in each asset class  
D. The custodian used for each holding  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Asset location places heavily taxed assets in tax-advantaged accounts and tax-efficient assets in taxable accounts, without changing the allocation.

*Reading: Private Wealth Pathway — Preserving the Wealth*

</details>

### Question 5

A lifetime gift is most likely to dominate a bequest when the recipient has:

A. A higher tax rate and a short horizon  
B. A lower tax rate and a long horizon  
C. The same tax rate and a short horizon  
D. A higher tax rate and a long horizon  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** A lower recipient tax rate and a longer compounding period both raise the relative value of gifting today rather than transferring at death.

*Reading: Private Wealth Pathway — Transferring the Wealth*

</details>

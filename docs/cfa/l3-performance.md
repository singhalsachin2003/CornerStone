# Performance Measurement

*CFA® Level III, published weight 5–10%.* Attribution, appraisal, manager selection and the GIPS standards.

## What the outline covers

- Portfolio Performance Evaluation
- Investment Manager Selection
- Overview of the Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS)

## Sample questions

These 5 questions are free in the app too. Try each one before opening the answer.

### Question 1

A manager overweighted a sector that outperformed the benchmark but selected stocks within it that underperformed. Brinson attribution will show:

A. Positive allocation and positive selection  
B. Positive allocation and negative selection  
C. Negative allocation and positive selection  
D. Negative allocation and negative selection  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Sector weighting decisions land in allocation; within-sector security decisions land in selection. The two can and often do have opposite signs.

*Reading: Performance Measurement — Portfolio Performance Evaluation*

</details>

### Question 2

An investor allocates to one of many specialist managers within a diversified programme. The most appropriate appraisal measure is:

A. The Sharpe ratio  
B. The information ratio  
C. Total return  
D. The Sortino ratio using total risk  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** For a benchmarked sleeve within a larger programme, active return per unit of active risk — the information ratio — is the relevant measure.

*Reading: Performance Measurement — Investment Manager Selection*

</details>

### Question 3

Hiring a manager who subsequently proves to have no skill is best described as:

A. A Type I error  
B. A Type II error  
C. A style drift  
D. A benchmark misfit  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** The null hypothesis is that the manager has no skill. Hiring anyway is rejecting a true null — a Type I error.

*Reading: Performance Measurement — Investment Manager Selection*

</details>

### Question 4

Under the GIPS standards, a firm claiming compliance must:

A. Include all fee-paying discretionary portfolios in at least one composite  
B. Include only its flagship strategies  
C. Present a minimum of three years of history  
D. Exclude terminated portfolios from historical composites  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** Firm-wide compliance requires every fee-paying discretionary portfolio to sit in a composite. Terminated portfolios must remain in the historical record.

*Reading: Performance Measurement — Overview of the GIPS Standards*

</details>

### Question 5

Which type of due diligence failure most commonly causes catastrophic investor loss?

A. Investment due diligence  
B. Operational due diligence  
C. Benchmark selection  
D. Fee negotiation  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** Fraud, valuation failure and inadequate controls — operational issues — account for most total losses, despite investment analysis receiving more attention.

*Reading: Performance Measurement — Investment Manager Selection*

</details>

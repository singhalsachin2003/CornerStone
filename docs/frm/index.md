# FRM® topic map

The next published sitting in this app is Part I on 14 November 2026. Each topic area below lists its published weight band and the learning modules from the outline, with free sample questions and answers.

## Part I

Tools — 100 questions, four topic areas

- [Foundations of Risk Management](/CornerStone/frm/p1-foundations/) — 20%. Risk taxonomy, governance, CAPM, multifactor models, financial disasters.
- [Quantitative Analysis](/CornerStone/frm/p1-quant/) — 20%. Probability, hypothesis testing, regression, time series, volatility, simulation.
- [Financial Markets & Products](/CornerStone/frm/p1-markets/) — 30%. Institutions, market structure, futures, swaps, options, rates, FX, MBS.
- [Valuation & Risk Models](/CornerStone/frm/p1-valuation/) — 30%. VaR and ES, option valuation, bond risk, stress testing, credit and op risk.

## Part II

Application — 80 questions, six topic areas

- [Market Risk Measurement & Management](/CornerStone/frm/p2-market/) — 20%. VaR and ES estimation, backtesting, EVT, GARCH, copulas, FRTB.
- [Credit Risk Measurement & Management](/CornerStone/frm/p2-credit/) — 20%. Merton and reduced-form models, credit VaR, counterparty risk, CVA, CDOs.
- [Operational Risk & Resilience](/CornerStone/frm/p2-operational/) — 20%. RCSA and loss data, model risk, cyber, third parties, financial crime, Basel.
- [Liquidity & Treasury Risk](/CornerStone/frm/p2-liquidity/) — 15%. Funding vs market liquidity, LVaR, transfer pricing, IRRBB, LCR and NSFR.
- [Risk & Investment Management](/CornerStone/frm/p2-investment/) — 15%. Factor theory, risk budgeting, attribution, hedge funds, illiquid assets.
- [Current Issues in Financial Markets](/CornerStone/frm/p2-current/) — 10%. This cycle's readings — AI in finance, climate risk, digital assets, bank stress.

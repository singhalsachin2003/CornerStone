# Cornerstone

A study app for CFA® and FRM® candidates. Snapshot cards, five-question sessions and a
spaced-repetition review queue — all on-device, with no account and no network requests.

- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)

or sponsored by CFA Institute or the Global Association of Risk Professionals.

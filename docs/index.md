# Cornerstone

A study app for CFA® and FRM® candidates. Snapshot cards, focused question sessions and a
spaced-repetition review queue — on-device, with an optional account that backs your
progress up.

Everything the app shipped with is free, permanently. An optional subscription opens the
study segments added since, and anyone who installed the app before the subscription
existed keeps all of it.

- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
- [Delete your account](DELETE-ACCOUNT.md)

## Questions people ask before installing

### Is Cornerstone free?

Yes, and permanently. Every topic area, snapshot card and question bank the app shipped with
is free with no account and no time limit. An optional subscription opens the study segments
written since, and anyone who installed the app before the subscription existed keeps those
too, permanently.

### Does it work offline?

Yes. All content ships inside the app, so a session on the Underground or on a plane behaves
exactly like a session at home. Nothing is fetched to read a card or answer a question.

### Do I need an account?

No. The app is fully usable without one. An account exists only to back your progress up so a
new phone does not start you over, and you can delete it from inside the app at any time —
see [Delete your account](DELETE-ACCOUNT.md).

### Which exams does it cover?

The CFA® Program — Levels I, II and III, including the Level III pathways — and the FRM®
certification, Parts I and II. 38 topic areas in total, each organised by the published topic
outlines and exam weight bands.

### Is it affiliated with CFA Institute or GARP?

No. Cornerstone is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, authorised by,
endorsed by or sponsored by CFA Institute or the Global Association of Risk Professionals.
